Trigger Direction

Control whether triggered animations play on entry, exit, or both. Uses trigger (entry), trigger-exit (exit only), or trigger-both (entry + exit).

fade-up() trigger

Items fade up on entry — stay visible on exit. <lay-out animate="fade-up() trigger" lg="columns(3)">

fade-up() trigger-exit

Items animate only when scrolling out of view. <lay-out animate="fade-up() trigger-exit" lg="columns(3)">

fade-up() trigger-both

Items fade up on entry, reverse on exit. <lay-out animate="fade-up() trigger-both" lg="columns(3)">

zoom-in() trigger-both

Items zoom in on entry, reverse on exit. <lay-out animate="zoom-in() trigger-both" lg="columns(3)">

flip-up() trigger-exit

Items flip only when scrolling out of view. <lay-out animate="flip-up() trigger-exit" lg="columns(3)">

Stagger shorthand — stagger

The same simple fade/slide/zoom reveals, written with the shared stagger vocabulary instead of animate="fade-up()". Scroll-driven by default — progress is tied to scroll position, so it re-runs every time you scroll back and forth. Add trigger for a one-shot that plays once and stays (Chrome 145+; older browsers keep the re-running reveal). The richer animate= effects above (flip, bounce, deep, exit, stack, pace, easing) have no stagger token and stay as animate=.
Token map: rise→fade-up · fall→fade-down · lft→fade-left · rgt→fade-right · zom→zoom-in · fde→fade-in · blr→blur (no animate= twin). For horizontal carousels see overflow.html.

stagger (default = rise, scroll-driven, re-runs)

Each card fades up as it scrolls through the viewport — scroll back up and it reverses. <lay-out stagger lg="columns(3)">

stagger="rise trigger" — one-shot (plays once, stays)

Same reveal, but trigger plays it once on entry and does not reverse on scroll-back. <lay-out stagger="rise trigger" lg="columns(3)">

stagger="zom"

Cards scale up from the inline-start. <lay-out stagger="zom" lg="columns(3)">

stagger="blr"

Cards sharpen into focus — no animate= equivalent. <lay-out stagger="blr" lg="columns(3)">
