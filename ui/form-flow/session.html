UI: Components > FormFlow > Sessions

The loan form with session.js: a partly filled form is saved and resumed, and a sent form cannot be opened again. There is no backend yet, so a mock server keeps its data in localStorage.

  • Reload the page: your answers come back (from sessionStorage, this tab only). The CPR number is marked privacy: "special" and is never saved, so type it again.
  • Save on this device keeps the draft after the tab is closed, for 30 days. Not on a shared computer.
  • Email me a link keeps the draft on the server. The link works once; it appears in the mock inbox below instead of an email.
  • Send the form, then press Back, reload, or look at a second tab of this page: it shows the receipt, not the form.
  • Signed in: the draft is kept on the server for 90 days after the last change, one per form.
