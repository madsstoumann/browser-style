UI: Components > FormFlow > Texts that follow the answers

A fine calculator whose rule set has named conditions and calculations, and no rules at all. The texts are written on the form: a note on a choice (pick a THC level, or Nitrous oxide or Other), a text block with cases (Sanctions lists every case whose named condition holds), and Show this field when (the THC level only for cannabis, the license question only for a medium level, the course and test fees only above a low level). Open it in the form editor.

The form also says how it is shown, in structure.present: { "mode": "page", "submit": false }, so it is one page with no Send button. The costs and sanctions are in a result panel (a layout entry of type panel, placed aside), beside the questions when there is room and below them when there is not. Try the same form as one page that unfolds, or one step at a time; the panel stays in every mode.

Tracking

A calculator sends nothing, so once the answers rest it reports form.result with the choices marked "track": true. The income and the totals are left out.
