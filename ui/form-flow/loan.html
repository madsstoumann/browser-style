UI: Components > FormFlow > Loan

The advanced example. Everything here comes from the form and the rules, plus example check functions the page passes in.

  • Variables: age is computed from the CPR number, a debt ratio from income and debt, and a credit score adds up from rules. Open Why to watch them.
  • Cross-field rules: under 18 is refused; the amount may not exceed five times the yearly income; the start date cannot be in the past (and warns more than a year ahead).
  • Score: below 40 adds a Documentation step and removes the 10 and 15 year terms.
  • Funnel tracking: every step, error, tracked rule and service call becomes an ff:track event, also pushed to window.dataLayer. Events carry ids only; the purpose and term choices are the two values marked as safe to track.
  • Backend: once CPR and amount are known, a partner bank (a static JSON file) is asked, and Next waits for it. It caps the amount at 300,000. A co-applicant gets their own step from another service.
