UI: Play

Web Component

Using <ui-play> as a registered custom element. Clicking the button toggles the is-playing state (aria-pressed on the inner button), swaps the inner <ui-icon> between type="play" and type="pause", and dispatches a bubbling ui-play-toggle event. With for, it also controls a <video>.
