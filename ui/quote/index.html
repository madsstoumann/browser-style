Quote

A quotation from another source: a <ui-quote> wrapper around a native <blockquote>, with the look chosen by a variant attribute — same shape as <ui-accordion variant>.
The <cite> element indicates the source of the quotation. A bare <blockquote data-variant> also works. No JavaScript.
