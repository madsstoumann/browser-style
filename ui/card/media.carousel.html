UI: Media — Carousel

CSS-only <ui-media> carousel: a scroll-snap row with native ::scroll-marker dots + ::scroll-button() arrows, driven by a media= token string. Native in Chromium; elsewhere /polyfill/carousel.js (JS off → plain swipe scroller).

Carousels in <lay-out> elements

The <lay-out> element is also a carousel host. Place whatever content within a lay-out — add the overflow attribute to enable a carousel.

<lay-out overflow media="pages" stagger> — flat cards

Flat <ui-reveal> cards; media="pages" snaps per page, stagger="rise" reveals each as it swipes in. Use block/no-translate effects (rise/fall/fde/zom/blr) — inline slides break scroll-to-end.

13 · Full-screen center carousel — bleed + preview-2xl center

A <lay-out overflow> hero: center centers the slide with 25% peeks each side (preview-2xl), bleed goes edge-to-edge, gaps adds end gutters. Controls: centered dots + a bare arrow set in the light/white ink variants (media="… arw(set) arw(bare) arw(lgt) mrk(bc) mrk(lgt)") so they read on the dark theme="black ink" band. Cards are responsive by aspect — portrait on mobile, cinematic 16/9 once wide (media="asr(3/4) md:asr(16/9)").

14 · Peek both sides — frame in a card (side by side)

Two framed carousels in a <lay-out lg="columns(2)">, each a <ui-card variant="bdr bdr(lgt)"> — a light hairline instead of a themed fill. Inside each card a <lay-out-group> holds a small header (eyebrow + title + one line — see section.html) above its swipeable sub-cards. frame is like center — a peek on each side — but start-snaps with a leading pad, framing a page of cards between symmetric peeks, advancing one card at a time. Arrows sit at their default center-edge position (nav(arw)) and dead-end arrows auto-hide (arw(hid)). Both use a thin preview-xs peek — left frames one card (columns(1)), right frames two (columns(2)).

Play/pause control — <ui-play>

<ui-play> in an autoplay carousel toggles the timer — sticky-pinned, placed with play(bs) / play(be). variant="reveal" hides it until hover/focus.

Two places to write it — media= on the host or on the frame

One token vocabulary, two placements: on the <ui-card> host (left — inherits to its <ui-media>) or directly on the <ui-media> itself (right). Identical render. Inheritance stops at the card host — a media= on a wrapping <lay-out> configures only the layout's own scroller.

Card border — variant="bdr"

A ui-card uses the surface background, so its edges can vanish against a surface page even with the shadow. bdr adds an opt-in hairline. Shades: bdr(lgt) (super-light) · default · bdr(drk) (darker). Widths: bdr(sm) 1px · bdr(md) 2px · bdr(lg) 3px. Author overrides: --ui-card-border-color / --ui-card-border-width / --ui-card-border-style (e.g. dashed).
