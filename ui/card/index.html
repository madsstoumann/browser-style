UI: Card

A CSS-first card engine. <ui-card> is the host; its two areas are the media area (<ui-media>, configured with media=) and the text area (<ui-content>, configured with content=). Composition — arrangement, overlay, theme, corners — is variant= on the host. Light DOM, no Shadow DOM, no JavaScript required.

This page is an index. Every demo below lives in demo/; the prose docs live in docs/.
