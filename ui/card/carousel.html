UI: Media — Carousel

The CSS-only <ui-media> carousel — a flex scroll-snap row with native ::scroll-marker dots and ::scroll-button() arrows, no JavaScript. Driven by the media= token string: nav() controls, arw() glyph/placement/size/bare, dot() pills, and nav(below) control bands. Chromium-only; elsewhere it degrades to a plain swipe scroller.

See also: media.html (the <ui-media> frame) · index.html (the full engine) · content.html (the <ui-content> side) · media.md (token reference).

Carousel — media="nav"

Add the nav token to put multiple sources in a scroll-snap row. By default it shows CSS-only navigation — dots (::scroll-marker) and prev/next arrows (::scroll-button()) — with no extra markup and no JS. Chromium-only for now; elsewhere it degrades to a plain swipe scroller.

Carousel controls — nav()

Pick which controls show by folding them into the token: bare nav (both), nav(dots), nav(arrows), or nav(none). nav(none) leaves a bare swipe scroller — there is no separate gallery variant; a carousel with its controls off is the gallery.

Carousel theming

Dots and arrows are token-driven. Recolour dots with --ui-media-dot-*. Each arrow is a circular button — colour the circle with --ui-media-arrow-bg (default semi-transparent black) and size it with --ui-media-arrow-size. The chevron is white by default (for the dark circle); for a light circle switch the glyph to dark with arw(dark). Square off the circle with --ui-media-arrow-radius.

Carousel arrows — arw()

Switch the arrow glyph with arw(arrow) (full arrow, shaft + head) — default is arw(chevron). Place the edge arrows vertically with arw(top) / arw(bot) (default arw(mid)). Group both into an adjacent pair with arw(set) (pair it with arw(bot) for a bottom-end set).

Carousel bare arrows — arw(bare)

arw(bare) drops the circle and renders the glyph itself as a coloured arrow — set the colour with --ui-media-arrow-color (default white over an image; auto-flips dark under nav(below)). A subtle --ui-media-arrow-shadow keeps a white glyph legible over bright photos. Composes with arw(arrow) and every placement atom.

Carousel pills — dot(pill)

Swap the round dots for pills with dot(pill). The active pill fills left→right over --ui-media-autoplay (default 5s) as a timer hint — it restarts whenever the active slide changes. JS-driven autoplay (which advances the slide on that cadence) lands later; the indicator is wired now. Under reduced-motion the active pill simply shows filled. Recolour with --ui-media-pill-track / --ui-media-pill-fill.

Carousel below the media — nav(below)

nav(below) moves both controls off the image into a reserved bottom band (dots centered, arrows at the ends) — the media keeps its full frame above. Size the band with --ui-media-band (default 2.75rem) and colour it with --ui-media-controls-bg. Combine with arw(set) to pin the dots to the start and pair the arrows at the end, or dot(pill) for a timer bar. (Arrow placement atoms arw(top/bot) are for the overlay variant; nav(below) owns its own vertical placement.)

Dot position in the band — dot(start) · dot(center) · dot(end)

In a nav(below) band the dots default to centered. dot(start) aligns them to the inline-start (after the left control), dot(end) to the inline-end (before the right control or the arw(set) pair). Shown here with arw(set) — the arrow pair stays bottom-right while the dots move left → center → right.

Vertical carousel — axis(y)

axis(y) turns the carousel vertical — a column scroll-snap row, snapping on the Y axis. The same nav() controls apply: dots become a vertical column on the inline-end edge, the arrows become up/down (the single base glyph, rotated) at top & bottom of that edge. arw(start) moves them to the other edge; arw(set) stacks the pair at the bottom; arw(bare) and the sizes all compose. Give the frame a portrait asr() so there's height to scroll.

Plain scrollbar — nav(bar)

nav(bar) drops the dots/arrows and shows the native thin scrollbar as the only affordance — works on either axis, and looks platform-correct out of the box (scrollbar-color: auto). Override colours with a single --ui-media-scrollbar-color token ("<thumb> <track>", e.g. #ccc transparent) and width with --ui-media-scrollbar-width (thin default).

Multiple items per slide — cols()

Wrap a group of items in a <ui-slide> element — that group becomes one slide laid out as a grid, and the carousel snaps a whole group at a time (one dot per group). cols(2) / cols(3) / cols(4) set the column count (default 2); gap via --ui-media-slide-gap. Items can be plain <img>/<video>, or full layered <ui-card>s (see below). Shown full-width.

Cards as slides — content below media

A <ui-slide> group can hold full <ui-card>s, not just images. Here each card is a standard variant="col" card (image with eyebrow / headline / summary below) — a product-grid carousel that snaps a whole row at a time.

Layered cards as slides — content on media

For text overlaid on the media, give each card the ui/reveal pattern: variant="ovr(bl)" + media="asr(3/4) obp(cc) scm" (aspect + object-position + automatic scrim) on the card. The carousel never leaks into the nested cards' own <ui-media>.

Bare <ui-media> — no <ui-card>

Every configuration works on a standalone <ui-media media="…"> too — the media= tokens drive it directly, no <ui-card> or <cq-box> needed. Two complex setups below. (If you need border-radius/overflow, put it on a wrapper — never on ui-media itself, that beats the component's :where() rules and breaks the scroller.)
