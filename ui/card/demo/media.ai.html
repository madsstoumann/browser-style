UI: Card — AI disclosure

<ui-ai> labels AI-generated and AI-modified media for the EU AI Act (Art. 50). The element's content is the text — GENERATED or MODIFIED by default, written by the renderer from the media item's ai fields, or any short phrase the CMS passes — and an empty element shows the icon alone. The parent media= says how it looks: ai(<cell>), ai(<hue>), ai(lg|xl). The icon is the Commission's own, as a CSS mask. Icon and word are the whole legal duty; the details ride aria-description, for screen readers only.

What is required, case by case

Only a deep fake must be labelled — AI content that shows or imitates something real. Row 1 (no AI) renders nothing. Row 8 (artistic, not real) is the same as rows 2–3. Rows follow the truth table in ui/ai/data/cases.json; a required label always carries its word — the renderer never writes it empty.

Word, phrase or icon

The content decides. Text shows the EU icon plus that text — the default word or a short phrase; an empty <ui-ai></ui-ai> shows the icon alone. The icon's alt text puts "AI" in the accessible name either way. Icon only is for voluntary labels — a deep fake keeps its word.
