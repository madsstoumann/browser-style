UI: Card — AI disclosure

<ui-ai> labels AI-generated and AI-edited media for the EU AI Act (Art. 50). The element's own options= says what is true — written by the renderer from the media item's ai fields — and the parent media= says how it looks: ai(<cell>), ai(label|icon|pill), ai(<hue>), ai(lg|xl). The icons are the Commission's own, as CSS masks. Icon and word are the whole legal duty; the details ride aria-description, for screen readers only.

What is required, case by case

Only a deep fake must be labelled — AI content that shows or imitates something real. Row 1 (no AI) renders nothing. Row 8 (artistic, not real) is the same as rows 2–3. Rows follow the truth table in ui/ai/data/cases.json.

Faces — and the legal floor

ai(icon) shrinks a voluntary or artistic label to the EU disc; the word stays in the markup for screen readers. A required label ignores it: icon and word are what the law asks for, at first exposure.
