UI: Card

Every card composes <ui-media> + <ui-content> inside a <cq-box> grid layer. The media frame is configured with media=, the text column with content=, and the composition (arrangement, overlay, theme, corners) with variant=. No JavaScript.

Themes — theme="… glass"

The glass modifier turns any theme colour into a material: a translucent fill plus a backdrop blur and saturation boost. With no colour token it is a neutral frost that takes its hue from whatever it sits over — hence the photo behind this section. glass composes with pale, muted, ink, dark and border (which draws the specular rim and, unlike a plain border, keeps the fill). Reach for pale glass whenever text is involved — a bare green glass puts the hue's ink on a translucent version of that same hue. That goes double for badges, which always show their ink. Note this stage pins color-scheme: light: pale mixes toward --color-surface, the page surface, so a region whose real backdrop is a bright photo has to say so — that is what the light/dark modifiers are for. theme="blue pale glass ink" — text takes the base colour, never the paired white. Pair ink with pale over busy imagery, or the hue sits on itself.

Card border — variant="bdr"

A ui-card uses the surface background, so its edges can vanish against a surface page even with the shadow. bdr adds an opt-in hairline. Shades: bdr(lgt) (super-light) · default · bdr(drk) (darker). Widths: bdr(sm) 1px · bdr(md) 2px · bdr(lg) 3px. Author overrides: --ui-card-border-color / --ui-card-border-width / --ui-card-border-style (e.g. dashed).
