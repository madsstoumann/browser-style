Carousel — mrk(dyn) prototype

Instagram-style dots: past 5 slides the dot strip becomes a fixed window — always 3 dots at normal size (the leading 3 on the first slide, the current dot and its trail mid-list, the trailing 3 on the last slide), the rest stepping down toward the edges. CSS-only: the ::scroll-marker-group is capped to 7 dot-pitches and auto-scrolls the current marker; each ::scroll-marker scales on its own view(inline) timeline.
