UI: Card — Recipe App

The schema.org Recipe card, exactly as the reference page renders it: its headline is a cover link and the steps are a Start cooking button (the teaser carries no instructions — they live on the page), both pointing at a dedicated page. That page is the kitchen app, built the way the product and listing pages are: one static render of the same data (recipes/build.js), so the card morphs into the page hero with a cross-document view transition. The ?id= is a route mimic — a CMS would resolve it; here it namespaces the page’s remembered state.

What to try on the page

  • Manual transport — the bar at the bottom: back · step N / 6 · play/pause · next, plus wake lock, copy and share. Everything works by touch; nothing needs a voice.
  • Cook mode — a fullscreen popover that grows from the button (@starting-style + transition-behavior: allow-discrete), one large step at a time, Esc or × to leave. Screen Wake Lock switches on while it is open — the pill reflects the real sentinel, not the toggle.
  • Timers — every step with a duration has one; wall-clock based, several can run at once, a three-note chime when done, and they keep running if you close cook mode.
  • Listen — play/pause reads the current step from its recorded file (script in recipes/narration.md), falling back to the Speech API for any step without one; headset and lock-screen controls (Media Session) play, pause and skip steps.
  • Hands-free — say “next”, “back”, “repeat”, “pause”, “ingredients”, “timer” or “close”. The mic mutes itself while the page is speaking.
  • Servings and units — scale 1–24 servings; switch metric ↔ imperial. Only the visible text changes: the recipeIngredient microdata rides a <data value> and stays as authored.
  • It remembers — current step, ticked ingredients, servings, units and voice, per ?id=, in localStorage.
