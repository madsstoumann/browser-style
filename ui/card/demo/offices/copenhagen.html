Visiting

The studio sits at the end of the quay, five minutes from Nordhavn station. Reception is on the ground floor; ring the bell marked Studio and someone will come down.

Visitors are welcome during opening hours. If you are coming for a workshop, bring a laptop — we supply everything else, including more coffee than is strictly advisable.
