Season 3: Sky Fortress

Four raiders, one collapsing sky. Pixel Raiders is a co-op dungeon crawler where every floor is stitched together the moment you drop into it — the same seed, the same rooms, on every platform you own.

The Sky Fortress season lifts the whole run above the cloud line: twelve new floating dungeons, a class that fights with weather, and a shared progression track that follows your account rather than your console.

Quests

  • The Cloudbreak — Climb out of the drowned quarter and reach the first floating dungeon.
  • Ballast and Bone — Cut the anchor chains holding the Fortress to the seabed.
  • The Long Static — Survive nine floors with the storm rising a level each time you rest.
  • Sky Burial — Return the Lantern Witch's compass to the room it was made in.

Classes

  • Stormcaller — Weather class — calls lightning down the vault lines, weakest in the open.
  • Ironvow — Shield-first raider who takes the hit meant for anyone standing behind them.
  • Lantern Witch — Sees one room ahead, and can hold a door shut for exactly ten seconds.
  • Rook — Scout who trades armour for a second dodge and a longer reach.

Gear

  • Aether Compass — Points at the nearest rift instead of north.
  • Stormglass Lantern — Burns brighter the closer the storm gets — and blinds you at full strength.
  • Raider's Sigil — Carries one death per run back to the party.
