Demo — Quiz — Quiz · stack-accordion

Quiz Flashcards

Quantum computing — flashcards

Three cards on the vocabulary every explainer assumes you already know. Open a card to check yourself.

Subject: Quantum computing · 3 cards · self-paced

What is a qubit?

The quantum unit of information. Unlike a bit it can hold a superposition of 0 and 1, and only collapses to one of them when measured.

What does quantum entanglement mean?

Two or more qubits share a single state, so measuring one instantly fixes the outcome of the others — however far apart they are.

Why does a quantum computer need error correction?

Physical qubits decohere in microseconds. Many of them are combined into one logical qubit whose errors can be detected and corrected faster than they accumulate.

Steps

Open the bleed valve

Wait for water

Demo — Glossary — DefinedTermSet · stack-accordion-divided

DefinedTermSet Glossary

Design-system glossary

The words this documentation uses in a specific sense, so a sentence means the same thing on every page. Four of them below.

Design token

A named value — a colour, a spacing step, a duration — stored once and referenced everywhere, so a change lands in one place instead of two hundred.

Container query

A style rule that reacts to the width of an element's own container rather than the viewport, which is what lets one card work as a hero and as a sidebar item.

Cascade layer

An explicit priority band in the cascade. Rules in a later layer beat earlier ones regardless of specificity, which removes most reasons to write !important.

Light DOM component

A custom element that keeps its children in the ordinary document tree instead of a shadow root, so page CSS, form submission and search engines all still see them.
