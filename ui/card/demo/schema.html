 HowTo Home maintenance

Bleed a radiator in four steps

Cold at the top, warm at the bottom? Trapped air. Ten minutes and a radiator key fix it.

Takes 10 min · ~€5 · Easy

  • Cloth
  • Small bowl
  • Radiator key
Steps
Turn the heating off

Switch the central heating off and let the radiator cool completely.

Open the bleed valve

Hold the bowl under the valve and turn the key counter-clockwise a quarter turn — you'll hear a hiss.

Wait for water

When the hissing stops and a steady trickle of water appears, all the air is out.

Close and check pressure

Close the valve, turn the heating back on and top up the boiler pressure if it dropped below 1 bar.
