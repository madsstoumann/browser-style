UI: Card — Phone shop

The colourway carousels from media.carousel.html, dressed as product tiles on a Danish telecom webshop. One coloured dot per slide picks the colour; the furniture sits on the media's 9-code grid, one cell each so nothing collides:

  • chip(ts) — <ui-chip> Nyhed! top-start, where the eye lands first.
  • sticker(te) — <ui-sticker> SPAR 20% splash top-end, where telecom shops put the campaign.
  • save(be) — <ui-save> wishlist heart bottom-end, by the thumb on a phone.
  • beacon(ts) — <ui-beacon> for live status (Kun i dag, Få på lager). It shares ts with the chip, so a tile carries one or the other.
  • <ui-ai> AI-genereret — on a narrow tile the bottom edge belongs to the dots and the heart, so the label moves into the content, right under the product name. A wide tile has room for it on the image: ai(bs).

The dots stay bottom-centre (nav(mrk)). Static specs — 5G, eSIM, Dual SIM — are plain chips in data-part="tags": they never change, so they don't need the beacon's motion, and they don't cover the product. Rating, price, stock and the call to action live in <ui-content>.

Product grid

Each tile adds only the furniture it needs: a brand-new model gets the chip, a campaign the sticker, a time- or stock-limited offer the beacon, an AI render the label. The heart is on every tile.
