UI: Card — <ui-content>

Everything that controls the content column: the scl() typographic scale (a decoupled body + headline ramp), the thm() themes, every data-part, pad() padding, spl() column splits, rds() corners, scrollable content, and the core surface tokens. All driven by variant / content tokens — no JavaScript.

See also: index.html (the full engine) · media.html (the <ui-media> side) · ui-card-tokens.md (token reference).

Typographic scale — scl()

content="scl(sm | md | lg | xl)" (default md) sets two container-driven clamp() ramps at once — a gentle body scale (eyebrow / summary / meta / tags) and a more aggressive headline scale. Decoupling them means a big card gets a display-size title while the body copy stays readable. Both also grow/shrink with the card's own width (cqi).

scl(sm)

Small & tidy

Body and headline both step down. Good for dense grids and metadata-heavy cards.

 scl(md) — default

The comfortable default

The baseline ramp used when no scl() is set.

 scl(lg)

Larger headline

Headline jumps ahead of the body — note the gap widening between the two ramps.

 scl(xl) Display

The hero tier — the headline goes display-size while the summary stays around 1.2rem.

The same scale on a wide card shows how hard the headline ramp pushes at scl(xl) while the body stays readable:

scl(xl) · wide container A display headline that scales with width

Resize the window: the headline tracks the card's own inline-size via cqi, bounded by its clamp(). The summary barely moves — that is the decoupled ramp doing its job.

Themes — thm()

Theme tokens remap the surface, ink, eyebrow and tag colours. The same card in default, thm(subtle), thm(dark) and thm(brand). Every value is overridable (e.g. --ui-card-dark-bg, --ui-card-brand-ink).

Arrangement & split — row / spl()

Beyond col (default), content can sit beside the media (row), before it (col-r / row-r), and the column ratio is set with spl(1/1 | 1/2 | 2/1 | 1/3 | 3/1).

Overlay content — ovr()

ovr(pos) stacks the content over the media and places it with the 9-code grid. Overlaid text defaults to white (set --ui-card-overlay-ink for light images). Pair with scm for a scrim — see media.html.

Squircle corners — rds(<size>-sq)

rds(sm-sq | md-sq | lg-sq | xl-sq) switches the host to a superellipse corner-shape and a matching radius — the smooth "squircle" corner. (Chromium-only; degrades to a normal radius elsewhere.)

Scrollable content — content="scr"

Add content="scr" to cap the content height and make it scroll, with a CSS-only fade-shadow on the masked edges (a scroll-driven animation). Cap the height with --ui-content-scroll-bs.

scr

A long read

Scientists have made a breakthrough in quantum computing that could change everything we know about processing power.

The implications stretch across cryptography, materials science and drug discovery — fields long bottlenecked by classical compute.

As you scroll, the top and bottom edges fade via a mask animated on a scroll-timeline — no JavaScript.

When you reach the bottom, the trailing fade lifts so the final line is fully visible.

Resize the card and the cap still holds at --ui-content-scroll-bs.
