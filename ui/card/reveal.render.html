UI: Card — Reveal Demo, Rendered

This page recreates ui/reveal/index.html section-by-section — but every card is built at runtime by render.js from UCF data files in data/demo/. The looks come from presets.demo.reveal.json merged over the base card.presets.json; sections that share content but change the look simply swap the preset reference. Back panels are either derived from the card's own envelope or come from a referenced flipside card. All DOM via createElement/textContent — no innerHTML.
