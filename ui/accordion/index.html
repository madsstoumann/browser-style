UI: Accordion

CSS

<ui-accordion> is a CSS-first collapsible content panel built on native <details> and <summary> elements. Use the name attribute for exclusive open behavior, change layout with types like horizontal, slide, and split, and compose variants like bordered, breakout, divided, rounded and separate.

This page demonstrates the CSS-only approach (no JavaScript).

See the web component demo for the JS-enhanced version using <ui-accordion-item>.

To reset your password, go to the login page and click on the "Forgot Password" link. You will be prompted to enter your email address associated with your account. How long does shipping take?
The shipping time depends on various factors such as your location, the shipping method chosen, and the availability of the product. Please note that these are just estimates, and actual delivery times may vary. You can track your order using the provided tracking number.
