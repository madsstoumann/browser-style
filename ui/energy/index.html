UI: Energy label

Standalone <ui-energy>: the EU energy label's nested display as a visual indicator. type= picks the scale the regulation sets for the product group, grade= the class, and the colour follows the class's place on that scale. On a card it is furniture with energy(…) tokens — see the phone demo.

Next to the price

The arrow is em-based, so inside the price it takes the price's font size, as the regulation requires.
